# 📚 Veritaso

> **Learning isn't about finishing chapters.  
> It's about proving you truly understand them.**

An AI-powered personalized learning platform designed to help learners organize their study journey, validate their understanding through intelligent quizzes, and build knowledge with confidence one concept at a time.

---

## 📖 About Veritaso

Most learning platforms focus on completing lessons.

**Veritaso focuses on understanding them.**

Instead of marking a task as complete immediately after studying, Veritaso asks learners to prove their understanding through AI-generated quizzes.

Only after demonstrating sufficient understanding does the learning journey continue.

This creates a learning experience based on **mastery rather than completion**.

---

## 💙 Why I Built Veritaso

Whenever I sat down to study, I often felt overwhelmed.

Questions like **"What should I study next?"**, **"Am I following the right path?"**, and **"How do I know if I actually understand this topic?"** would constantly distract me from learning itself.

I found myself switching between multiple applications—one for notes, another for tasks, another for quizzes, and yet another to track my progress. Instead of helping me stay focused, it made studying feel fragmented and exhausting.

I wanted a single place that could guide my learning journey, organize everything I was working on, test my understanding, and motivate me to keep moving forward.

That's why I started building **Veritaso**.

Veritaso started as a project for my resume, but quickly became the application I wished I had while preparing for interviews and learning new technologies.

My goal isn't to create just another study application. I want to build a learning companion that helps students stay focused, measure their understanding, and genuinely enjoy the process of learning.
